About Us

Yatraa was built on a simple idea: planning a trip should feel as good as taking one. We were tired of juggling spreadsheets, sticky notes, and a dozen browser tabs every time we booked a flight — so we built one home for every itinerary, budget, and memory.

What you get

With Yatraa you can plan your entire trip, all in one place.

Section-by-section planning

Split trips into flights, stays, and activities, each with its own dates and budget.

Calendar view

See every trip laid out on a monthly calendar so nothing overlaps by accident.

Trends & budgets

Track spending and see where your travel time actually goes, trip after trip.
