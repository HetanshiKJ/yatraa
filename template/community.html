Just got back from an amazing 5-day trip to Kyoto! The Fushimi Inari Shrine at sunrise was absolutely worth waking up early for.
Highlights: • Fushimi Inari Shrine • Arashiyama Bamboo Grove • Nishiki Market • Traditional tea ceremony
Travel tip: Visit the popular temples early in the morning to avoid the crowds. Also, try staying in a traditional ryokan for at least one night—it made the trip feel much more authentic!
