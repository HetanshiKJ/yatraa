About Yatraa

Our Story

Every great trip starts with a plan — and somewhere along the way, that plan usually ends up scattered across five different apps. We built Yatraa because we were tired of losing track of confirmation numbers, forgetting what we'd budgeted for, and re-explaining our itinerary to travel companions over text.

Yatraa brings it all together: section-by-section itinerary building, a calendar that actually shows your whole trip at a glance, and budgets that update as you plan. Whether it's a weekend getaway or a six-country backpacking trip, Yatraa keeps the details organized so you can stay present for the parts that matter.
